TECHNOMATIC 2105 v30 - musical source and asset audit

Purpose

The owner reported that some older Velvet Circuit renditions resembled a
Zelda treasure/secret cue. No seed, recording or time range for that example
was provided. This audit examined the supplied v28 project, the v29 baseline,
the current production source and the actual v30 Android packages for imported
musical content, and reviewed the synthesis/arrangement paths relevant to
the reported sparkling sound. It is not an audio-similarity search.

The current inventory is reproducible:

    --apk /absolute/path/technomatic_2105_v30_debug.apk \

The automated inventory detects file assets and explicit API/name references.
It does not establish what an arbitrary encoded data table represents, prove
the historical authorship of every formula, or compare music perceptually.

Manual music-path review

Pitched audio is synthesized in MusicEngine.cpp from oscillators, envelopes,
harmonic relationships and generated instrument parameters. Percussion is
synthesized from short pitched/noise attacks. Scale-degree arrays and tonal
intervals provide general musical grammar; phrase generation, mutations and
theme transforms create the note sequences. No imported commercial recording,
soundfont, MIDI song or transcribed commercial tune was identified.

The native fopen path writes rendered PCM for export; it does not read a sound
asset. Java export code writes OGG/FLAC and their metadata. Oboe 1.10.0 supplies
Android audio I/O and is declared in app/build.gradle; it is not a music bank.
Existing LICENSE and NOTICE attribution is retained. General synthesis and
music-theory building blocks are used; originality here does not mean claiming
to have invented scales, oscillator synthesis or all musical relationships.

Changes addressing the reported resemblance

The former Velvet Circuit channel and all other channel profiles are retired.
Legacy selection preferences and recipe channel fields cannot reactivate them.
The unrelated internal StyleType name VelvetDrift is an existing synthesis
starting point, not the removed Velvet Circuit channel.

The older ornament scheduling contained regular high sparkling attacks, such
as fixed sixteenth positions 3/7/11/15, and recurring bell placements. Old lead
models also included strongly chiming inharmonic partial relationships. These
are plausible contributors to the reported resemblance, not confirmation of
the missing recording's cause.

v30 replaces those repeated ornament lattices with generated, phrase-related
pitched replies and revises chiming families toward harmonic instrument tones.
Instrument-family selection stays stable rather than changing with incidental
color offsets. Foreground exchanges and generated arrivals provide musical
drama without importing a reward cue or adding a special-effect layer.

Scope of the conclusion

The inspected project contains no identified imported commercial musical
content. New v30 devices and synthesis families were implemented from the
project's generated grammar and mathematical synthesis, without fetching or
transcribing a commercial reference. That finding does not guarantee that no
short generated phrase can ever resemble existing music. The specific reported
older output cannot be reproduced or judged without its seed or recording.
