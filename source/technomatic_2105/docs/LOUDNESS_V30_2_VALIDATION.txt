TECHNOMATIC 2105 v30.2 - Final loudness validation
Date: 2026-10-20
Final calibration: G=1.75, K=0.40, C=1.00; engine=30; outputrev=2.
These are host measurements, not a phone, street, earbud or listening test.

1. RESULT

Across the same 16 seeds used for the v30.1 loudness survey, settled output
rises by a median paired 4.50 LU and 4.519056 dB RMS. Individual settled
loudness increases range from 3.7 to 4.7 LU. The population median changes
from -14.05 to -9.60 LUFS. A median of paired differences is not necessarily
the difference between the two medians.

The quietest sampled settled case, seed 3711502885, changes from -16.4 to
-11.7 LUFS. The loudest sampled case, seed 3668339987, changes from -10.1 to
-6.4 LUFS. The entire 88-second renders gain a median paired 4.45 LU; their
first 10 seconds gain a median paired 4.50 LU.

The linear-region gain is 20*log10(1.75)=4.86076097 dB. Strong peaks receive
less increase because the stateless curve rounds them. The sample ceiling
remains 1.0, with no lower app cap. The final native maximum observed sample
is 0.998284101486 (-0.014917 dBFS); all 135,168,000 measured channel samples
are finite and inside [-1,1]. No gain detector or moving volume control is
introduced.

2. FIXED SAMPLE AND METHOD

The sample is unchanged from the earlier declared v30.1 survey: one initial
representative per slow/groove/driving/intense intent and twelve lower-note-
density candidates selected from the previous musical development audit.
It is a finite stress sample, not the quietest possible seed set or an
exhaustive guarantee across all seeds, edits, instruments and devices.

Each seed is rendered from zero for 88 seconds at 48,000 Hz stereo. The
native observer writes float WAV without normalization, integer clipping
or codec conversion. RIFF and file sizes are verified. Each release has
1,408 generated seconds and 135,168,000 float channel samples. Three windows
are measured separately: [0,88), [0,10), and [40,88) seconds.

FFmpeg ebur128 with peak=true supplies integrated LUFS, loudness range and
estimated reconstructed peaks. Complete command lines, FFmpeg versions and
meter logs are retained. Its displayed LUFS and true-peak figures are rounded
to 0.1 unit. RMS and maximum native sample magnitude are measured directly
from float PCM. Stereo RMS includes musical rests.

3. CALIBRATION AND QUALITY TRADEOFF

The first sweep held the old 0.80 knee and full 1.0 ceiling while comparing
fixed gains 1.0, 1.5, 1.75, 2.0 and 2.25. Median settled increases were 0,
3.45, 4.75, 5.75 and 6.60 LU respectively. Gain 1.75 was selected for its
substantial increase before the increasingly strong peak reshaping needed
for the smaller additional gains at 2.0 and 2.25.

The boosted 1.75/0.80 candidate exposed an important codec tradeoff: the
intense Opus excerpt had 153,344 decoded channel samples above full scale,
3.328% of its 4,608,000 samples. Two wider knees were therefore compared,
without changing the gain or ceiling:

Knee  Settled median gain  Intense Opus overfull samples  Intense Opus dBTP
0.80       +4.75 LU                   153,344                     +2.0
0.60       +4.65 LU                    68,293                     +1.9
0.40       +4.50 LU                    22,254                     +1.4

The final 0.40 knee sacrifices only 0.25 LU of the median boost relative to
the first 1.75/0.80 candidate, while reducing that measured intense Opus
overfull count by about 85.49%. The fraction of intense settled native
samples at magnitude >=0.99 falls from about 3.146% to 0.031%.

K is the start of gradual rounding, not a new output cap. C stays 1.0. The
wider curve affects more samples, but rounds them more gently and produces
fewer near-flat peaks. In the final settled sample the median fraction of
samples in its nonlinear region is 21.07%, with a maximum of 38.69%.
Nonlinear sample count alone therefore does not rank audible quality.

The retained calibration also reports RMS of (F(x)-G*x) divided by RMS of
G*x. For the final settled set this relative waveform residual has median
7.534% and maximum 16.983%. It compares against an intentionally unbounded
linear amplification; it is not THD, a listening score, or a claim about
human audibility. This is stronger fixed mastering, not distortion-free
amplification. No ears-on or universal quality claim is made.

6. LOSSLESS AUDITIONS AND REMAINING CODEC LIMITS

The comparison archive contains four matched before/after pairs: slow seed
1013904226, groove 2654435761, quiet driving 3711502885, and intense
3668339987. Each is the same [40,88) second excerpt, 48 kHz stereo PCM16.
All eight FLAC files decode exactly to the intended PCM conversion, covering
36,864,000 PCM16 channel samples. There is no level matching, extra fade,
ReplayGain tag or added normalization. A/B filenames identify the releases.

Host Opus checks use 128,000 bit/s and 20 ms frames, matching the app's
requested bitrate and frame size. OpusHead output gain is zero. This is
host libopus, not an Android MediaCodec implementation. Final results:

Seed        Intent          Decoded samples >1.0   Percent    Opus dBTP
1013904226  slow                          209      0.00454       +0.2
2654435761  groove                         54      0.00117       +0.1
3711502885  quiet driving                  67      0.00145       +0.2
3668339987  intense                    22,254      0.48294       +1.4

Each row contains 4,608,000 decoded channel samples. The intense decoded
maximum magnitude is 1.1724215. Final native reconstructed peaks reach
+1.4 dBTP across complete renders and +1.3 dBTP in settled excerpts, even
though every stored native sample is bounded. A decoder/resampler or integer
playback stage can clip reconstructed overrange.

The gentler knee reduces overshoot versus the rejected louder 0.80-knee
prototype. It does NOT establish less distortion or overrange than delivered
v30.1: the old intense Opus case had 803 overfull samples (0.0174%). The
additional average loudness still has a peak-shaping/codec tradeoff. This
release does not claim universal codec-clipping-free output and does not
silently reduce the owner's requested 1.0 ceiling.

8. PRACTICAL SCOPE

This is a volume-only revision. It preserves v30.1's UI, 20-entry history,
playback behavior and memory model. No scanner, rewind or persistent naming
features are included. Android system volume remains under the listener's
control. The measurements establish a substantial source-audio increase but
cannot promise parity with RFS/FM radio or audibility in a particular street,
phone, headphone, Bluetooth route or manufacturer audio processing path.
