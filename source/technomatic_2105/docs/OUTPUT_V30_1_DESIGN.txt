TECHNOMATIC 2105 v30.1 - Output design
====================================

Purpose
-------
This revision makes two changes: it permits the full digital sample range,
and it removes automatic or composition-driven changes to the band's volume.
Android and the listener continue to control the system playback volume.

1. Fixed output and full digital range
-------------------------------------
MusicEngine no longer contains the RMS AGC (mAgcRms/mAgcGain), mMaster
attenuation, kick-driven mSidechain ducking, or mTransitionGain. The old
fade/dead-air state machine and its timers are removed. Legacy next() now
switches immediately; the app's forceNewPiece() path remains immediate.
Export no longer applies a final tailGain fade. It converts exactly the
requested [Start, End) interval, including its final samples.

The original per-song static drive and fixed DC filter remain to preserve
the existing tone. Drive is selected at generation/import and does not
evolve. The DC filter has ordinary filter history, not a level detector or
automatic gain control.

OutputStage.h applies this fixed transfer independently to each channel.
Here x is the sample after the retained drive and DC filter:

    G = 1.0                 fixed gain
    K = 0.80                knee
    C = 1.0                 digital sample ceiling
    z = G * x

    F(x) = z                                      if abs(z) <= K
    F(x) = sign(z) * [K + (C-K) *
             tanh((abs(z)-K)/(C-K))]               otherwise

Value and slope meet continuously at the knee. Samples below the knee
remain linear; higher peaks are smoothly rounded. There is no output
detector, attack/release controller, lookahead, normalization, added latency,
buffer, or persistent stage state. A loud sample cannot change a later
sample's gain or impose gain reduction on the other channel.

The source sample range is bounded by [-1.0, 1.0], with no lower app ceiling.
This is a sample bound, not a reconstructed true-peak guarantee. Resampling
or codec reconstruction can produce intersample peaks above digital full
scale. The requested full-range setting is retained without an adaptive
controller or additional attenuation.

2. No coordinated section-volume envelopes
------------------------------------------
The nine scheduling amplitude sites are kick, snare/clap, closed hats,
open hats, percussion, bass, harmony, foreground, and supporting voices.
Their positive amplitudes retain fixed role levels, existing user/support
weights, and individual accents. Section and device volume multipliers
have been removed from all nine sites.

The former phraseDrum/Bass/Lead/ChordScale variables are now named
drumActivity, bassActivity, leadActivity, and chordActivity. Their existing
arithmetic, note conditions, probabilities, and RNG calls are retained for
scheduling. They do not continuously scale played-note amplitudes.

Only structural presence reaches the amplitude path:

    Presence(A) = 1.0 if A > 0.0, otherwise 0.0

These binary masks preserve previously silent events at completed endings,
including forced anchors. They cannot create a gradual volume swell or dip.
Intro/outro reveals, Hook boosts, Shadow/OpenRelease dips, Confluence gain
accents, and long-term level drift therefore no longer change the band's
note levels together. Their scheduling and melodic devices remain.

Individual instrument attack/decay/release envelopes, per-note accents,
rolls, flams, rests, and fixed role balance remain part of the music.
An exact export cut can end on a nonzero waveform; no file-edge fade is
added to conceal that cut.

Recipe and switching consistency
--------------------------------
Snapshots retain engine=30 and add outputrev=1. Old or missing outputrev
fields do not select a quieter output path: all recipes use the current
fixed delivery processing.

A switching defect could retain the previous song's mPocketLate backbeat
delay. generateSeededSong() now derives that timing from a separate local
RNG using the same initial draws as reset(). This preserves reset/load
timing and the composition RNG streams while making both Next paths agree
with a fresh load of their saved recipe.
