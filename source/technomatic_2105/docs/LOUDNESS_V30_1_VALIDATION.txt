TECHNOMATIC 2105 0.30.1 - FIXED OUTPUT VALIDATION
Date: 2026-10-20
Authoritative final-candidate report; host measurements, not a device audition.

1. OUTCOME

The final candidate uses fixed unity output gain, permits the full +/-1.0
sample range, and has no master AGC, sidechain ducking, output gain envelope,
lookahead limiter, automatic normalization, or whole-mix fade. The existing
per-song drive and DC-removal filter remain. A stateless smooth curve rounds
only samples above magnitude 0.80 and approaches magnitude 1.0.

The user also requested removal of coordinated phrase/section volume changes.
Nine scheduled-amplitude sites now use only a binary part-presence mask in
place of the former continuous phrase multipliers. The old continuous values
still determine note probabilities, gates, entrances and exits. Individual
note envelopes, accents, fixed part balance, selected instrument weights,
roll/flam accents, music grammar and instrument selection remain.

The 16-seed final sample is substantially louder than delivered v30. On the
settled 40-88 second excerpts, the median paired increase is 5.85 LU and the
median RMS increase is 6.08 dB. Paired loudness increases range from 4.0 to
7.2 LU. Median integrated loudness changes from -19.65 to -14.05 LUFS. The
median of paired changes is intentionally distinguished from subtracting
the two population medians.

The complete 88-second renders gain a median 5.95 LU; startup windows gain a
median 6.0 LU. No extra gain was added beyond removing the former attenuation
and volume modulation. The quietest settled seed in this declared sample,
3711502885, improves from -21.2 to -16.4 LUFS.

All native float samples are finite and within full scale. The largest
observed sample is 0.99839127 (-0.01398 dBFS). Reconstructed true peaks are a
separate matter: FFmpeg estimates up to +0.4 dBTP across full renders, and
host Opus decoding reaches +0.7 dBTP in the intense audition. This release
therefore does not claim universal reconstructed-peak or codec clipping-free
output. The requested full-scale ceiling has not been reduced to create
additional headroom.

3. MEASUREMENT METHOD AND SAMPLE SELECTION

A portable C++17 observer was compiled against the exact archived v30 source
and the final source with g++ -O2 -Wall -Wextra -Werror. Each of 16 fixed seeds
was rendered for 88 actual live seconds from time zero, at 48,000Hz stereo.
The observer writes unnormalized 32-bit float WAV without clipping or codec
conversion. Complete RIFF/file size is checked before every measurement;
new outputs are renamed from a temporary path only after successful close
and size verification.

Each version therefore supplies 1,408 generated seconds and 135,168,000 float
channel samples. The full 0-88s, startup 0-10s and settled 40-88s windows were
metered separately. RMS is the square root of mean squared values across both
channels and includes musical rests. Sample peak is the largest absolute
float sample. Integrated loudness and estimated true peak use the installed
FFmpeg ebur128 filter with peak=true. Its reported loudness/true-peak values
are rounded to 0.1 unit; sample peak and RMS are also measured directly from
the float files. The FFmpeg version and complete meter logs are retained.

The selection was fixed before loudness metering: the first known
representative of each of four intents plus three lower-primary-note-density
candidates per intent from the previous v30 development audit. Those twelve
candidates are a useful sparse-case stress sample, not a claim to be the
quietest possible seeds. This is a finite sample, not an exhaustive bound on
all seeds, edited recipes, devices or codecs.

The new final curve is exactly linear at unity gain for magnitudes <=0.80.
Across each complete 88-second final render, the fraction of samples above
that knee has median 0.0271%, minimum 0.000237% and maximum 0.9235%. Thus even
the most affected sampled tune keeps over 99.07% of samples in this curve's
linear region. This describes the new final curve, not equality of final
PCM to the old release: the removed AGC/ducking and phrase-volume macros
intentionally change the audio level.

4. FIXED-OUTPUT AND COMPATIBILITY CHECKS

All final checks below passed against the source hashes in section 2.

- 58 focused native checks, plus a source absence check for retired AGC,
  master attenuation, ducking and fade fields. The final stage is an empty
  stateless type. A 200,001-value sweep verifies bounded, monotonic, symmetric
  transfer; low-level input retains exact unity gain; the knee joins with a
  continuous first derivative. Earlier loud samples and opposite-channel
  peaks do not alter the transfer for a later sample.
- A direct quiet/loud/quiet input test verifies there is no slow gain
  recovery after a loud input. Only the existing DC-filter memory remains.
- Four intent representatives render identically in blocks of 1, 257, 1024
  and 4093 frames. Reset and saved-recipe reconstruction are sample-exact.
  Fully muted voices remain exact zero. Host 8kHz, 44.1kHz and 96kHz runs
  also remain finite and bounded.
- Both public next() and the app's forceNewPiece() path switch immediately,
  reset elapsed time, and match the new displayed recipe's fresh first two
  seconds sample-for-sample. This found and fixed an inherited stale timing
  value: mPocketLate could carry over from the prior song. A seed-local RNG
  now recreates reset's timing draw without consuming performance RNG.
- Four finite range exports of [3,12) seconds have the exact requested frame
  count. Every PCM16 sample, including the final 50ms, matches an independent
  render configured with the same finite-export arrangement. Repeated
  exports are byte-identical. There is no additional export fade. A finite
  export still has its musical ending, so its later arrangement is not
  asserted to equal the endless live arrangement.
- 10,124 controlled-accent kick and foreground-lead events keep constant
  positive amplitude within each seed across Intro, Hook, Shadow,
  OpenRelease and both conclusive/dissolving Outro modes. The actual
  scheduler continues to choose probability, entrances, exits and roles.
- The existing v30 native regression gate passes 64 checks, including its
  three-minute live render, bounded drum mutation, channels' retired API
  behavior, recipe/history behavior, fixed palette and finite export.
  Only the obsolete transition-state assertion was replaced by direct
  no-automatic-ending and immediate-next behavior checks.
- AudioEngine control regression: 93 checks passed with host Oboe/log stubs.
- Edited-recipe regression: 12,582 checks passed; 48 cases, 144 successive
  reloads, all 40 override fields, and 23,040,000 exactly equal float samples.
  These are exact replay claims within this final host build, not a claim
  that the old release's quieter mixed waveform is preserved.

5. MUSICAL IDENTITY CHECK

For all 16 seeds, a separate bounded scheduler observer compares 64 phrases
against archived v30. All 20,247 scheduled events retain onset offset, event
kind, pitch/frequency, duration, pan, color, foreground/owner fields and
instrument slot. Both performance and development RNG states are included
after each step. All 16 score/RNG signatures match exactly. Full-event hashes
that include amplitude differ for all 16, as intended by the requested
removal of coordinated phrase-volume changes.

The observer drains events/voices each step. It is a structural scheduler
check and does not simulate every live voice interaction. The separate
88-second renders exercise real PCM synthesis. The source diff was also
reviewed by the engine and root agents. No claim of mathematically exhaustive
musical identity across all possible inputs is made from this finite probe.

use slow seed 1013904226, groove 2654435761, quiet driving 3711502885 and
intense 3668339987. Every file is an exact [40,88) second excerpt, 48kHz

8. PRACTICAL LIMITS

No claim of ears-on listening, Android hardware output level, street-noise
intelligibility, a particular headphone sound pressure, or subjective audio
quality is made here. Android build/package checks are documented separately.
System volume limits/settings are not bypassed or changed by these host tests.

Fixed unity gain does not make every song, note or rest equally loud. Different
instruments, pitches, note envelopes, densities and arrangements retain their
natural signal differences. Those musical differences are separate from the
removed automatic gain and coordinated phrase-volume controls.
