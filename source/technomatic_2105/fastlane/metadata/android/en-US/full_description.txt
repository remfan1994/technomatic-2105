Technomatic 2105 generates original synthetic music directly on your Android device.

Version 30.2 increases average output loudness with a stronger fixed calibration, shared by live playback and exports. Android media volume remains the listener's control. The app adds no automatic gain riding, whole-mix ducking, volume fades or coordinated part-volume swells and dips.

One composition develops indefinitely until you explicitly choose another seed. Recognizable themes, complementary statements and answers, rhythmic variation and generated arrivals keep the music related as it develops. Slow, relaxed, driving and intense music all come from the same generator.

Version 30 expands the available instrument vocabulary while preserving version 29's ensemble roles and per-song support-count rule. A fixed instrument palette is chosen for each composition from a larger catalogue of bass, lead, harmony and percussion synthesis families. No sound files, sample packs or commercial patches are loaded.

Channels are discontinued. The full generator is available without a named-channel selector. Old channel preferences cannot narrow the music or reactivate a retired profile.

Phrase planning coordinates theme recall, development, foreground exchanges and arrivals. New musical devices alter relationships between existing voices and phrases. Slow compositions receive attention to register, phrase continuity and cadence spacing. The engine retains v29's intentional rests and bounded drum development.

The main player includes Start/Stop, elapsed time, Previous/Restart/Next, and a latest-20 listing with seed and listened duration. Advanced provides seed loading, Start/End timeline export to OGG or FLAC, and a separate metadata editor. Export is independent of live playback.

OGG and FLAC embed metadata inside the actual audio file, including the seed, engine version, user notes, export interval and full regeneration recipe. The automatic seed identity is ready when the Metadata Editor opens. Existing user metadata remains editable.

Public Music export requires Android 10 or newer; playback supports Android 8 or newer.

Privacy and freedom properties:

* no advertising SDKs

* no trackers
* no external sound assets
* no sample packs

* no background hiss beds, noise/zap effects or delay processing
