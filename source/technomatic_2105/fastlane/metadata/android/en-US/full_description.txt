Technomatic 2105 generates synthetic electronic music directly on your Android device.

The default is No Channel and one indefinitely evolving sound. No Channel leaves the generator unrestricted. The current seed remains fixed until the listener explicitly presses Next or loads another seed/history entry. There is no automatic sound replacement or live duration boundary.

Named Channels provide optional predictable character without loading stored music. Changing Channels reinterprets the current seed and restarts the same composition from 0:00; it does not create a new seed. Each Channel mixes approximately 50 percent Channel character with 50 percent unrestricted generation: Chrome Pulse, Velvet Circuit, Glass Trap, Dust Machine, Liquid Grid, Neon Drift, Broken Speaker, Deep Magnet, Pixel Ritual, Soft Voltage, Heavy Orbit, and Cold Arcade. Hybrid Channels can combine several, with the first selected Channel remaining dominant.

All pitched layers are generated from symbolic musical grammar rather than selected as finished note sequences. Each sound receives newly generated harmony, lead, hook, verse, bass statement and answer, counter-line, arpeggio, pulse, and ornament material. Composition memory preserves recognizable returns while bounded development gradually changes the sound.

Tension is generated per sound rather than imposed through one universal pad rhythm. The engine uses seed-specific omission, convergence, hinge, shadow, afterimage, and rare short pad-breath devices.

A generated timbre grammar selects among broad electronic bass, lead, pad, drum, articulation, and pad-voicing families for each sound. The result remains seed-stable and musically related while giving different sounds a wider performed identity.

The main player includes Start/Stop, Channel selection, elapsed time, Previous/Restart/Next, and a latest-20 listing with Channel, seed, and listened duration. Advanced provides seed loading, Start/End timeline export to OGG or FLAC, and a separate metadata editor. Export is independent of indefinite live playback.

* no trackers
* no external sound assets
* no sample packs
* all sound synthesized locally
